# NNNN - Short title

## Status (Statut)

Proposed | Accepted | Rejected | Superseded by NNNN. Date: YYYY-MM-DD.

## Context (Contexte)

What forces are at play? What problem or constraint requires a decision?

## Decision (Décision)

What is decided, stated in the active voice ("We will ...").

## Alternatives considered (Alternatives considérées)

Each option that was weighed, with the reason it was not retained.

## Consequences (Conséquences)

What becomes easier, what becomes harder, what must be done next, what stays at risk.
